Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VI Clip Studio ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our Windows desktop application and this website. The App runs entirely on your own computer. We do not operate servers that receive, store or analyze your data.

1. Information stored on your computer

To work, the App saves the following information locally, in the App's data folder on your PC:

  • Your settings, including any API keys you choose to enter.
  • The recordings you add, the clips the App creates and your posting schedule.
  • For each TikTok account you connect: the account's open ID, display name, profile picture URL, the access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, and the permissions you granted.
  • Basic statistics about your own TikTok videos (see section 2).
  • Public trend data the App reads from YouTube and, if you enable it, Instagram (see section 3).

This information never leaves your computer except when the App sends requests to the services described below, on your behalf. We (the developers) do not receive a copy.

2. TikTok data

When you connect a TikTok account, you sign in on TikTok's own page using TikTok Login Kit, and you choose which permissions to grant. The App uses each permission only as follows:

PermissionWhat the App uses it for
user.info.basicReads your open ID, display name and profile picture so the App can show which account is connected.
video.uploadUploads the clips you approve to your TikTok inbox as drafts, so you can finish and post them in the TikTok app.
video.publishIf you choose direct posting, publishes the clips you approve and schedule, with the caption and privacy setting you selected.
video.listReads the title, cover image, link and view, like, comment and share counts of your own videos, to show their performance in the App.

The App sends to TikTok only the video files, captions and settings for the clips you approve. TikTok data is used only to provide these features. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising or combined with data from other sources about you.

3. Other services the App uses

  • YouTube API Services. The trend radar uses the YouTube Data API to read public information about videos (titles, channel names, publish dates and view, like and comment counts). It does not access your YouTube account. By using this feature you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Instagram Graph API (optional). If you enter your own access token, the App reads public posts for selected hashtags. This is subject to Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Anthropic API (optional). If you enter your own API key, the App sends trending titles and keywords and basic clip information (target market and type of moment) to Anthropic to write hooks and captions. It does not send your videos or account information. This is subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

4. Website

This website is a static site with no cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) as described in GitHub's Privacy Statement.

5. Data retention and deletion

  • Information stays on your computer until you delete it.
  • To disconnect a TikTok account, remove it in the App's Publishing section. This deletes its tokens and stored statistics from your computer.
  • You can also revoke the App's access at any time from TikTok's settings, in the section that lists the apps connected to your account.
  • To delete everything, delete the App's data folder or uninstall the App.

6. Security

Tokens and API keys are stored only on your computer and are hidden in the App's interface. All communication with TikTok and the other services uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Keep your computer and Windows account secure, because anyone with access to them can use the App.

7. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and you must meet the minimum age required by TikTok and the other services you connect. We do not knowingly process data of children.

8. Your rights

Because your data is stored on your own computer, you can access, correct or delete it at any time as described above. If you have questions about your data or want to exercise any privacy right that applies to you, contact us.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. Important changes will be announced on this website.
