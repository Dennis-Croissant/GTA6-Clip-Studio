Your best gameplay moments, ready for TikTok.

VI Clip Studio is a desktop app for Windows that finds the most exciting moments in your own gameplay recordings, edits them into vertical 9:16 clips and sends them to your TikTok account, so you spend less time editing and more time playing.

How it works

Four steps from a long recording to a short clip in your TikTok account. You stay in control of every clip.

1

Record

Save your gameplay recordings to a folder on your PC. VI Clip Studio picks up new files automatically.

2

Find the highlights

The app analyzes sound peaks and scene changes to find the moments with the most action.

3

Edit to 9:16

Each moment becomes a vertical clip with a title hook, your watermark, a progress bar and balanced audio.

4

Send to TikTok

Connect your TikTok account and send approved clips as drafts to your inbox, or schedule them to post at the times you choose.

Features

Built for gaming creators who publish short videos regularly.

Trend insights

See which topics, titles and video lengths are working right now, based on public video statistics.

Automatic editing

A consistent, retention-focused template for every clip, with text in English, Spanish, Portuguese or Chinese.

Review before posting

Preview, edit the hook and caption, and approve each clip before it goes anywhere.

Drafts or scheduled posts

Send clips to your TikTok inbox to finish them in the TikTok app, or schedule posts for the best hours in your audience's time zone.

Content rights built in

Every source is labeled as your own recording, licensed or used with permission. Material with unconfirmed rights is never published.

Private by design

The app runs on your computer. Your recordings, clips and account connection stay on your PC; we do not run servers that collect your data.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to common questions.

What do I need to use VI Clip Studio?

A Windows 10 or 11 PC, your own gameplay recordings and a TikTok account. The app installs its own video tools the first time it runs.

Does the app post without my approval?

No. Clips are only sent to TikTok after you approve them, or after you turn on automatic approval for your own recordings. You can send clips as drafts so you make the final decision inside the TikTok app.

What does the app do with my TikTok account?

It uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API to show your account name and picture, upload the clips you approve and read basic statistics about your own videos. You can disconnect at any time. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Can I use other people's videos?

Only with the creator's permission or a license that allows reuse, and the app adds the credit to the caption. We recommend using your own recordings. See our Terms of Service.

Is VI Clip Studio affiliated with TikTok?

No. VI Clip Studio is an independent tool that uses TikTok's public developer APIs. It is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by TikTok.
